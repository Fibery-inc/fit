# Public proof points

Checked 12 November 2026 against [fibery.com/customer-stories](https://fibery.com/customer-stories). These are the only customers the agent may name. Sizes are as the stories give them; the stories are undated and some are old.

## Rules

- Name a customer only if it is in this file, and link the story.
- Say only what the row says. Do not add numbers, user counts or outcomes.
- One proof point per answer at most, and only when it matches the visitor's segment or use case.
- For "how many companies like mine use it", use the use-case files in `use-cases/`, not this file.
- If no row matches, say there is no public story for that kind of company.

## Do not cite

## Gaps

There is no public story for consulting, construction and real estate, retail, non-profit, schools and universities, finance teams outside product companies, or health providers. Most stories are product companies of 11–50 people. When a visitor is from a segment with no story, rely on the use-case data and say no named example is public.
